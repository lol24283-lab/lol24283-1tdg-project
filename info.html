In our club we try to go on trips or experiences about every two weeks. These trips are optional but strongly recommended to go to as they are very educational on our club and what it is about. Some of our trips and experiences do have a cost to them, but generally we go on trips that are affordable.

Meeting dates: Students meet every Thursday in Hepburn 3.3 at Lunch

Upcoming Events:

13/08 : Japanese Chrome Car Show

23/08 : Visit to Car Mechanic in Cambridge

09/09 : Car Swapmeet at Car Museum

To join this club, contact us via the club's email carsclub123@gmail.com or via the contact phone number 021-345-6789. In your text or email, you must include your full name, phone number or email, what year you are in school, if you have any prior experience with cars or being in a car club and let us know a bit about you so when you come to the first meeting we know a bit about our new members.
