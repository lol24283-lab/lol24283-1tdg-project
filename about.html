About Page

Photo by Florinel ZONE on Unsplash

The Car Club is for everyone interested in cars. We participate in multiple activities and go on multiple Trips throughout the year. Students attend things like car swap meets, car shows, car racing. They also go on trips to old car mechanics and car experts. The car club is not only a fun club but a club of learning. Every time students attend a trip they are all told to find out specific things about cars or racing every time and we encourage students to take images of the cars and car parts with consent from the owners to present it at club meetings. On our Gallery page there are examples of photos that students in the club have taken at past events they have attended with or for the club.
